	Read the attached transcript carefully and create the complete YouTube packaging for this video.

	OUTPUT FORMAT

### TITLE OPTIONS

Provide 7 title options.  
Rules:

- Each title must be under 100 characters.
- No emojis.
- Natural YouTube titles, not clickbait spam.
- Prefer curiosity and story over generic SEO wording.
- Titles should reflect the actual central idea of the video.
- Avoid exaggerated claims that the transcript does not support.  
	After the seven titles, identify the title you think best represents the video and briefly explain why.

### DESCRIPTION

Write a 150–250 word YouTube description.  
The description should:

- open with the story rather than keywords
- sound like a real person describing this particular video
- explain what viewers will experience without giving away every conclusion
- reflect the tone of the transcript
- naturally include relevant Thailand/retirement context
- avoid generic promotional filler  
	Do not simply repeat the title.  
	At the bottom include:  
	💬 Join our Community Discord  
	[https://discord.gg/PFENZwaqZy](https://discord.gg/PFENZwaqZy)  
	❤️ Support us on Patreon  
	[https://www.patreon.com/c/chanyawazza](https://www.patreon.com/c/chanyawazza)

### CHAPTERS

Create YouTube chapters from the transcript.  
Rules:

- Use MM:SS format
- First chapter must begin at 00:00
- Generally create a chapter every 2–3 minutes
- Chapter names should describe meaningful shifts in the story
- Do not create chapters merely because a timestamp exists in the transcript

### HASHTAGS

Provide exactly 10 hashtags.  
Rules:

- Each must begin with #
- Put them on one line
- Separate them with commas
- Relevant to this specific video
- Do not stuff generic unrelated terms

### YOUTUBE TAGS

Provide YouTube tags as one comma-separated line.  
Rules:

- Entire tag string must remain below 500 characters
- Include these baseline tags where relevant:  
	thailand, bangkok, chanya and wazza, aussie living in thailand, aussie expat, warren gerdes  
	Then add tags specific to this video.

### THUMBNAIL / SPLASH TEXT

Provide 3 short thumbnail text options.  
Rules:

- Maximum about 2–5 words each
- Designed to be readable on a television
- One clear idea
- Do not simply repeat the video title
- Avoid cheap clickbait

### STORY ANALYSIS

Finally, give a short assessment of:

1. What you believe the video's real central story is.
2. What emotional or intellectual hook would make someone watch it.
3. What part of the transcript is strongest.
4. Anything in the video that may confuse viewers or weaken the story.  
	IMPORTANT  
	Do not search the web.  
	Do not ask follow-up questions.  
	Do not use information that is not contained in the transcript or the channel context above.
