WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

A useful companion.
A memory of your own.

Ariadne began with a simple ambition: keep knowledge beyond any single conversation, and make it available to the AI you choose. The Knowledge Vault holds the notes and evidence; Ariadne brings that memory together with chat, tools and creative work.

The approach is practical: preserve the source, show the evidence, keep control of the work, and test whether an idea helps in everyday use.

“Take AI into the garage and see if it actually works.”Garage Alchemy / Pope Kael TV

THE SYSTEM TAKING SHAPE

Four parts. One workspace.

Memory · connections · capabilities · interface
01 / MEMORY

Knowledge Vault

Durable Markdown knowledge, linked context and retrieval evidence. A second brain that can travel with you.

02 / CONNECTIONS

MCP

A shared way for different AI systems to reach the Vault and its capabilities, without handing them ownership of your memory.

03 / CAPABILITIES

Toolshed

Controlled tools for research, local services and production. Explicit actions, visible progress and inspectable results.

04 / INTERFACE

The workspace

Chat, cached news, creative work and model controls, brought together in a browser with the system’s state in view.

NOTES FROM THE BUILD

The thread so far

A few milestones, kept as the project grows.

  1. 10 OCT 2026

    Find something useful. Keep the thread.

    Rabbit Hole now looks for projects connected to our Windows and AMD workstation, local AI and media workflows, and practical Garage Alchemy experiments. Ten cards show the reasons to care, expandable project statistics and source evidence. Next ten continues through unseen projects; saved projects move to the Watchlist and leave discovery.

    The Watchlist keeps projects, broader web questions and recurring reminders until you decide otherwise. Check all now queues active source watches, while reminders keep their schedule. Discovery stays manual, cached results remain available, and a shared GitHub request budget provides early warnings and automatic pauses. Documentation can suggest a local test; whether it actually works still needs a trip into the garage.

  2. 09 OCT 2026

    The Lab builds, runs and checks its work

    The Lab toggle in Chat opens a local build workflow. The selected coding model can create an application, inspect its files, run it in the sandbox, test its controls and make targeted repairs from concrete errors. Saved conversations keep the request, result and build together.

    Workbench remains the development preview. A separate Lab Runner presents verified builds full screen with keyboard focus. Elapsed time, output activity and diagnostics make longer jobs easier to follow. A live counter build completed in four model calls, with repeated Add and Reset interactions checked in the browser. More demanding applications remain in testing.

  3. 02 OCT 2026

    Personal recommendations enter everyday testing

    A major step for Home: three simple reactions—useful, more like this and not interested—teach future suggestions. A local journal remembers changes and removals, unseen stories lead on refresh, and a Seen view keeps earlier reading within reach. Reactions and reading controls share one tidy row. A diversity pass leaves room for wider news.

    The first version is now in everyday use. Over the next few days, we’ll watch whether suggestions become more useful, repetition stays low and the feed keeps its breadth. The balance will be tuned from that experience.

  4. 02 OCT 2026

    A steadier place to read

    News cards now stay in place while background updates arrive. HTTPS launches, faster health reporting and recovery of Signal matching form the checkpoint before personal recommendations.

  5. 01 OCT 2026

    The Vault’s work becomes visible

    Visible ingestion controls and session recovery make long-running Vault work easier to follow and manage.

  6. 30 SEP 2026

    Follow the evidence

    Chat activity and source routing come together, making research progress and the sources behind an answer easier to follow.

  7. 28 SEP 2026

    News, ready when you are

    Cached articles and images give Home a dependable local starting point for reading and discussion.

  8. 15 AUG 2026

    One name for the wider system

    The project’s four parts—Vault, MCP, Toolshed and workspace—are documented together under Ariadne.
